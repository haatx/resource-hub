# Houston Arts Data Resource Hub

This is a public directory of research and data on Houston's arts sector. It collects studies, surveys, dashboards, maps, and other resources into one place. The collection includes research focused on artists, arts organizations, audiences, arts education, civic art, cultural districts, and the broad context around Houston's arts sector. It's assembled and maintained by HAA's Senior Data Analytics Manager S.R. Resources can be searched and filtered by access, place, focus, format, research method, and publication year. Each resource entry provides a summary, key finding when available, source and citation info, geographic coverage, methodology, publication date, update frequency, and the organization responsible for it. Hub is intended for artists, arts organizations, funders, civic partners, and anyone really looking for reliable information about the arts in Houston.

**Live site:** https://haatx.github.io/resource-hub/

## Using the Hub

The directory can help users:
* Find sourced stats for reports, arts programming, grant applications, advocacy and public discussions.
* Compare their data with Houston data, peer cities, and national research.
* Learn about audiences and participation across Houston.
* Get original data and research without having to trace a stat back through secondary sources.

Searches and selected filters are reflected in the page URL, making filtered views easy to share.

## Contributing

Ideas and suggestions for other resources that may belong in the hub are welcomed. Users can also report information that appears broken, mislabeled, out of date, etc. through the hub.
