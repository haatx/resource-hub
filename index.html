How to use the directory

Filter down, then open a row.

Every resource sits behind a single row. Open one and you'll get a plain-language summary, the key finding where the source reports one, and the details you need to cite it: who it covers, what methods it used, when it was published, how often it refreshes, and who maintains it.

The filters narrow the list six ways, and they combine; the search box runs across titles, authors, summaries, and stewards at the same time. Some options narrow further, opening a second set of choices beneath them when you select one.

Whatever you've filtered to is written into the address bar, so you can send someone the exact view you're looking at rather than telling them which boxes to tick.

Who's behind this

Built and maintained by Houston Arts Alliance.

Houston Arts Alliance assembles and maintains this collection, keeps the records current, and is the point of contact for anything on it.

The hub grew out of the community's need for a place to find and use research on Houston's arts sector. It's built for anyone making the case for the arts here, artists, arts organizations, funders, and civic partners.
